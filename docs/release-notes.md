# ZZNES 0.8.11 Beta

Application/launcher and AmiPkg version: **0.8.11-dev**.

- Battery saves for supported MMC1, MMC3 and MMC5 cartridges, up to 32 KiB.
- Verified replacement, retained backup and recovery after an interrupted rename.
- Trainer data and legacy save-state compatibility preserved.
- No change to audio synthesis, guest timing, clock settings or firmware.

[Save-file guide and supported boards](battery-saves.md) ·
[Validation](validation/battery-save/README.md).

## Installation

Extract the LHA and open Install. Choose a writable destination. Close the emulator
before updating; settings, ROMs and saves are preserved. MUI and AHI requirements
are checked. Clock controls require the optional clock service; emulation does not.
The archive includes the README, credits and licence documents.
